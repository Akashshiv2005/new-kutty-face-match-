# KuttyStory Search Engine Keyword Map

> **Policy Notice**: In strict adherence to white-hat SEO guidelines, search volumes, current rankings, and keyword difficulty scores are intentionally omitted as they cannot be verified in this offline environment. All keyword clusters are mapped strictly 1:1 to prevent internal keyword cannibalisation.

---

## 1. Keyword Architecture & On-Page Rules

Every indexable URL is assigned:
- **One Primary Keyword**: Must appear in the `<title>` tag (within 60 characters), the single `<h1>`, the first 100 words of visible page body copy, at least one `<h2>` heading, and the primary hero image `alt` attribute.
- **Secondary / Supporting Keywords**: Distributed naturally across `<h2>`/`<h3>` subheadings, feature cards, and contextual paragraph copy.
- **Natural Wording**: No keyword stuffing, hidden text, or grammatical distortion.
- **Spelling Strategy**: "Personalised" (British/Indian English) is the primary target; "personalized" (US English) is included as a natural secondary variant.
