## 5. Post-Delivery Review Collection & Feedback Architecture

KuttyStory already includes an internal feedback feature (`/admin/feedback` and user ratings). Reviews must be collected ethically and published strictly with verified parental consent.

### 4-Step Review Collection Workflow:
1. **Trigger**: Automated WhatsApp / Email message dispatched 3 days post-delivery:
   > *"Hi [Parent Name], we hope [Child's Name] loved opening their adventure in [Book Title]! Did you see the look on their face when they spotted their name? We'd love your honest thoughts."*
2. **Review Link**: Direct link to the existing rating/feedback modal or Google Business Profile review link.
3. **Explicit Consent Checkbox**:
   - `[X] I grant KuttyStory permission to display my review and first name on the website without revealing private contact details.`
4. **Publishing Rule**: Never fabricate or generate AI reviews. Only reviews with documented permission in the database may be rendered in `<section>` blocks or structured data schemas.
